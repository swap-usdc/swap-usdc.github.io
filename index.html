What is USDC

What you are swapping when you swap USDC

USDC is a dollar-pegged stablecoin issued by Circle and used on Ethereum as an ERC-20 token. People swap USDC to buy ETH or another token, to move between stablecoins such as USDT, or to go back to dollars after a trade.

Native USDC vs USDC.e

On some Layer 2 networks two versions exist: native USDC, issued directly by Circle on that chain, and bridged USDC.e, a wrapped copy created by a bridge. They can trade at similar prices but are different contracts with different backing paths. On Ethereum mainnet there is one USDC contract, so check the address before you trade.

How the swap works

A DEX router searches liquidity pools for a route, shows the expected output, and you sign the swap from your own wallet.

Stable vs volatile

USDC to a stablecoin vs USDC to a volatile token

Both are market swaps, but the risk profile differs. The price of the token you receive is what changes most.

Still a market swap

A USDC↔USDT swap is not a fixed 1:1 conversion. It is priced by pool balances, so the quote can differ from one dollar per token, especially during market stress.

How to swap

How to swap USDC

Route and quote

The interface asks DEX liquidity for the best route, then shows the expected output, price impact, estimated gas and your slippage tolerance. Nothing is signed until you confirm.

Step by step

  1. Connect your wallet on Ethereum and set USDC as the sell token and your target token as the buy token.
  2. Enter an amount and review the quote, route and estimated gas.
  3. Approve USDC once if this is the first time this wallet swaps it through the router.
  4. Sign the swap in your wallet and wait for it to confirm on-chain.
  5. Check that the bought token arrived at your wallet address.

To go back, reverse the pair: sell the token for USDC.

Fees & gas

What a USDC swap costs

Gas

Gas on Ethereum is paid in ETH, never in USDC. The one-time approval and the swap are separate transactions. See how gas works.

Trading cost

The quote already reflects pool fees, price impact and any aggregator fee. Your slippage tolerance sets the lowest output you will accept before the transaction reverts.

One-time approval

USDC is an ERC-20 token, so the router needs an allowance to move it. Later swaps from the same wallet reuse that allowance. Keep some ETH in the wallet, or you cannot approve or sign.

Security

Staying safe when you swap USDC

Non-custodial by design

You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself. The interface quotes and routes; it never holds your funds or keys.

Before you sign

  • Verify the USDC contract address against Circle's published list; fake USDC tokens exist.
  • On Layer 2 networks, confirm whether you hold native USDC or bridged USDC.e.
  • Approve only the amount you need and revoke old ERC-20 allowances you no longer use.
  • Check the quote, price impact and slippage before confirming.
  • A stablecoin can lose its peg and smart contracts can contain bugs, so risk is never zero.
FAQ

Swap USDC FAQ

Can I swap USDC for ETH without a centralized exchange?

Yes. This interface routes your USDC through on-chain DEX liquidity, and you sign the swap from your own wallet. No account is created and no funds are deposited with the interface.

Why do I need to approve USDC before swapping?

USDC is an ERC-20 token, so the router needs an allowance to move it on your behalf. The approval is a separate transaction that costs gas, and it is usually needed only once per wallet and router. Native ETH needs no approval.

Is swapping USDC to USDT always 1:1?

No. It is a market swap priced by pool balances, so the output is usually close to one-to-one in deep pools but can drift. Check the quote and price impact before you sign.

What is the difference between USDC and USDC.e?

Native USDC is issued by Circle directly on a given chain, while USDC.e is a bridged version created through a bridge. They are separate contracts, so verify which one you hold and which one the destination expects.

Which token pays gas when I swap USDC?

ETH pays gas on Ethereum. Holding only USDC is not enough, because you need ETH in the same wallet to approve and sign the swap.

Can I swap USDC for any ERC-20 token?

You can swap USDC for any ERC-20 token that has enough on-chain liquidity for a route. For thin or new tokens, expect higher price impact and verify the contract address first.
